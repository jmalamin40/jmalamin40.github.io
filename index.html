Case study & shipped products

Case study · Fintech / MCA · Current

UW True Advance: AI-based underwriting CRM

Automates the full Merchant Cash Advance (MCA) funding lifecycle, from lead intake and application review to offer, e-signed contract and funding.

Problem

Underwriting MCA deals means juggling a CRM, a deal tracker and contract tooling by hand. Every handoff is slow, error-prone and hard to audit.

What I built

  • Connector layer for GoHighLevel, MCA Tracker and DocuSeal
  • Decision engine that combines rules and AI to recommend approve, decline or an offer
  • Contract automation that generates contracts from deal data

Key design choices

  • Event-driven sync: webhooks, not polling, keep every platform consistent in real time
  • Rules + AI: clear rules handle the hard limits and AI handles nuance, so recommendations stay explainable
  • Status loop: signing events flow back into the CRM automatically

04 / Approach

How I engineer

The principles behind the systems I build.

01

Boundaries before code

Clear modules and contracts between services, so teams and features can grow without stepping on each other.

02

Events over polling

Webhooks and queues keep integrated systems consistent and responsive, and they're cheaper to run.

03

Automate the path to prod

Containerised builds, CI/CD and zero-downtime deploys, so shipping is routine instead of risky.

04

AI where it earns its place

Deterministic rules for hard constraints, LLMs for judgement, and the result is always explainable to the business.
